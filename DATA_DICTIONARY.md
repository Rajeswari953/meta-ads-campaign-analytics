# Data Dictionary

- date: campaign date
- campaign: campaign name
- ad_set: audience/ad-set group
- platform: Facebook or Instagram
- spend: advertising spend in INR
- impressions: ad impressions
- reach: unique accounts reached
- clicks: ad clicks
- conversions: recorded conversions
- leads: recorded leads
- ctr: click-through rate (%)
- cpc: cost per click (INR)
- cpm: cost per 1,000 impressions (INR)
- conversion_rate: conversion rate (%)
- cost_per_conversion: spend divided by conversions
- cost_per_lead: spend divided by leads
